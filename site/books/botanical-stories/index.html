The plant collection of Den Gamle By

The nursery was founded in the 1880s as a market garden. Today the museum’s gardeners grow the plants that once stood on windowsills and now fill the houses of Den Gamle By, along with vegetables and summer flowers. The parade greenhouse dates from around 1850 and first stood at Bernstorff Palace. Buildings like it were made to show the plant collections of their time. It also holds the museum’s historic potted plants, many of them tended for more than a hundred years, through several generations of owners. Source: Den Gamle By, Aarhus
